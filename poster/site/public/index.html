Tired Linguist Poster

A personal scheduling tool

Tired Linguist Poster is a private tool used by its owner, an independent game developer, to schedule and publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channels (The Tired Linguist, GravityTheWordGame and Limina Word Game) and, in future, the owner's own Instagram accounts.

It is not a public service. Only the owner's accounts are connected, and no other users can sign up.

Uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channels. By connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.
