Tired Linguist Poster

A personal scheduling tool

Tired Linguist Poster is a private tool used by The Tired Linguist to schedule and publish its own videos to its own social media accounts, such as its YouTube channel and Instagram account.

It is not a public service. Only the owner's accounts are connected, and no other users can sign up.
